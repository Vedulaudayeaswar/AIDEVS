# Acceptable Use Policy

> DRAFT — requires legal review before public launch.

You must not use AIDevs to create, request, host or distribute:
- Malware, phishing pages, credential-harvesting forms, or sites impersonating real organisations or people.
- Content that is illegal, infringes intellectual property, or exploits or harms minors.
- Harassment, hate, violent extremism, or content that targets private individuals.
- Spam, deceptive commerce, or fake reviews or records presented as genuine.

You must not:
- Attempt to access other users' projects or data, bypass authentication, sandboxing or usage limits, or test the service for vulnerabilities outside our [security policy](https://github.com/Vedulaudayeaswar/AIDevs/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- Use reference-website analysis to copy proprietary content, crawl sites that disallow it, or target internal networks.
- Automate account creation or send AI requests at a volume intended to exhaust shared capacity.
- Resell access to the platform's AI capacity.

We may remove content, limit usage, or suspend accounts that violate this policy. Report abuse to [contact email].
